# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Store owners and administrators use StaffBot Admin to manage employees, stores, income approvals, salary requests, attendance, leave, exports, and operational logs for Telegram-based staff workflows.

## Product Purpose

StaffBot V2 replaces a fragile Google Apps Script and Sheet workflow with a stable Cloudflare Worker plus D1 system. Success means admins can review and maintain operational records quickly, with clear auditability and low maintenance overhead.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a serious operations tool: restrained, clear, dense enough for repeated use, and never decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid landing-page styling, oversized cards, gradients, glass effects, playful decoration, heavy shadows, rounded novelty controls, and layouts that make dense operational data wrap into tall rows.

## Design Principles

- Keep operational data scannable before making it decorative.
- Use one consistent component vocabulary for forms, tables, navigation, and actions.
- Favor compact, stable layouts that reduce scrolling and avoid accidental wrapping.
- Reserve strong color for primary actions, current selection, and destructive actions.
- Preserve auditability and admin confidence over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, clear disabled/loading states, native controls where possible, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.
